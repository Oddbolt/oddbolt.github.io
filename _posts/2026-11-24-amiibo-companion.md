A collection tracker for amiibo collectors, for Android.

## Features

- Browse the full amiibo catalogue with search and filters.
- Mark what you own, including duplicates, and name each copy.
- Keep a wishlist with priorities.
- See your completion and stats by series.
- Find official store pages at Best Buy, Walmart, and Amazon.
- Optional detailed tracking: packaging, condition, price paid, notes, and photos.
- Everything stays on your phone. Export a backup any time.

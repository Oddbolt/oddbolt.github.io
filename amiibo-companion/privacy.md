## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Oddbolt ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Amiibo Companion mobile application for Android (the "App"). It describes what information is stored, what information is collected and by whom, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

The App is designed so that your collection data stays on your device. We do not operate user accounts, and we do not receive, store, or sell the information you enter in the App.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Summary

- **No account is required.** The App has no sign-up or sign-in.
- **Your collection stays on your device.** We never receive the information you enter.
- **We do not sell your personal information.**
- **Advertising is provided by Google AdMob**, which collects certain device information to show and measure ads, as described in Section 6.
- **Usage statistics are optional and off by default.** If you turn them on, anonymous usage counts are sent to Aptabase, as described in Section 7.

## 3. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following information only on your device, in storage that is private to the App:

- The amiibo you mark as owned and the details you add to each copy, such as packaging, condition, purchase date, price paid, notes, and copy names;
- Your wishlist and its priorities;
- Photos you attach to a copy;
- Your settings, such as theme and completion preferences;
- Downloaded copies of the amiibo catalogue, store links, images, and news.

This information is not transmitted to us or to any third party by the App.

**Device backup.** If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include your collection, wishlist, and settings (but not photos) in your Google account backup. When you move to a new device with Android's device-to-device transfer, photos may also be transferred. These backups are handled by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Device permissions

| Permission | When it is used | What happens to the data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera | Only when you choose to take a photo of a copy | Saved in the App's private storage on your device. Never uploaded. |
| Photos (system photo picker) | Only when you choose existing photos | Copied into the App's private storage on your device. Never uploaded. |

You can withdraw these permissions at any time in your device settings. The rest of the App continues to work without them.

## 5. Information collected automatically by third parties

Apart from the optional usage statistics in Section 7, the App does not collect analytics or personal information for us. The third-party services below receive certain information when the App connects to them. We do not receive or store this information.

| Service | Purpose | Information involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Google AdMob](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) | Shows and measures advertising | Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information, and app interaction and diagnostic data |
| [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) (oddbolt.github.io and raw.githubusercontent.com) | Hosts the amiibo catalogue, store links, news, and images that the App downloads | IP address and standard request information, as with any website |
| [Aptabase](https://aptabase.com/legal/privacy) | Optional usage statistics, only if you turn them on (Section 7) | The statistics described in Section 7; IP address used briefly to determine approximate country and region, not stored |
| [jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net) | Backup download source if GitHub is unavailable | IP address and standard request information |

**Links you open.** Store links (Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon) and news headlines open the retailer's or publisher's website or app outside the App. Those websites and apps are operated by third parties under their own privacy policies, and we do not control the information they collect.

## 6. Advertising

The App displays advertising provided by Google AdMob. Google may use the information described in Section 5 to show ads, measure their performance, limit how often an ad is shown, and prevent fraud. Depending on your Google settings, ads may be personalized based on your interests.

**Advertising ID.** The App uses your device's advertising ID, a resettable identifier provided by Google Play services, only through Google AdMob and only for advertising and ad measurement. We do not read it ourselves, link it to any other identifier or personal information, or send it with the optional usage statistics in Section 7.

Under some U.S. state privacy laws, the use of information for personalized advertising may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in your device settings (on most devices: **Settings > Privacy > Ads**). After you delete it, ads are no longer personalized using that ID.

More information: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 7. Optional usage statistics

The App can send anonymous usage statistics to help us understand which features are used and what to improve. This feature is **off unless you turn it on**. The App asks you once, after explaining what is sent, and you can change your choice at any time in **Settings > Privacy > Share anonymous usage stats**. Closing the question without answering is treated as "no".

**What is sent when it is on:** the screens you open (for example "Discover" or an amiibo's page, but not which amiibo), certain actions (such as choosing a Discover category, opening a store link with the store's name, or opening a news story), the time of each event, a random session number that changes after an hour of inactivity, the App version, your Android version, and your language setting.

**What is never sent:** your collection, wishlist, notes, prices, photos, or copy names; your advertising ID or any other device identifier; your name, email address, or any account information.

**Who receives it:** the statistics are processed by [Aptabase](https://aptabase.com/legal/privacy), an analytics service provider that counts them on our behalf. According to Aptabase, your IP address is used only briefly to determine your approximate country and region and is never stored, and the statistics are kept for up to five years. Because they do not identify you, we cannot link them to you or delete them individually. We do not sell these statistics, and we use them only to improve the App.

When you turn the feature off, the App stops sending statistics immediately and discards any that have not yet been sent.

## 8. Affiliate links

Some store links may be affiliate links. If you make a purchase after tapping one, the retailer may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. Any tracking is performed by the retailer or the affiliate program on its own website or app. The App does not send them your personal information.

## 9. Files you export or share

When you export a backup or a spreadsheet (CSV), the App creates a file and opens your device's share sheet. You decide where the file goes, for example Google Drive or Files, and that service's privacy policy applies. Backup files contain your collection, wishlist, settings, and photos; spreadsheet files contain your collection and wishlist.

## 10. Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use them only to respond to you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed for that purpose.

## 11. How we use information

We do not use the information stored in the App, because we do not receive it. Information you send us by email is used only to respond to you. Optional usage statistics, if you turn them on, are used only to understand how the App is used and to improve it (Section 7). Information collected by Google AdMob is used by Google as described in Section 6.

## 12. Data retention and deletion

Information stored in the App remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by removing items in the App, clearing the App's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the App. Because we hold no copy, uninstalling the App deletes it permanently, except for any backup files you exported or any Android backup described in Section 3.

Optional usage statistics are kept by Aptabase for up to five years in a form that does not identify you (Section 7). Information held by other third-party services is retained under their own policies.

## 13. Security

The App keeps your information in private storage that other apps cannot access. All data the App downloads is transferred over encrypted HTTPS connections, and downloaded files are checked before they are used. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we design the App to keep your information on your device and out of our hands.

## 14. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 15. Where the App is available

The App is offered in the United States. The third-party services described above may process information in the United States and other countries under their own policies.

## 16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version on this page. For significant changes, we will also post a notice in the App's News tab.
